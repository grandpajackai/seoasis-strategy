# A proposed first 90 days in the strategist seat

This sample plan starts at account kickoff. Every task is a proposal; account goals, current fulfillment, available access and client priorities determine final scope. SEOasis is the public methodology case study; merchant implementation and revenue fields require an actual client account.

**Operating objective:** translate the merchant’s commercial goal into a small, well-evidenced release, verify it independently, measure by cohort and make a documented next-quarter decision. No delivery quota, revenue target or 90-day payback guarantee is assumed.

## Cadence and planning rules

- Weekly operator review: delivery, QA, index/feed exceptions, early query signals, dependencies and next actions.
- Client update: concise work shipped, results, caveats and needed decisions; align to SEOasis’s public Friday/fortnightly cadence.
- Monthly commercial review: comparable Search/Shopping acquisition, purchases, net sales, stock, margin and funnel context.
- Day 90: keep, expand, revise or stop each hypothesis, with the client.
- Rank using impact × confidence ÷ effort; weights are configurable in the dashboard. Impact/effort are relative 1–5 judgments. P0 prerequisite gates and dependencies can override the mathematical rank.

Public reporting cadence: [SEOasis homepage](https://www.seoasis.io/) — accessed 14 October 2026; Friday reports, fortnightly strategy calls and quarterly recalibration are publicly stated.

## Day-90 review record

For each workstream, record the original hypothesis, release/cohort, baseline, outcome, constraints, confounders, evidence confidence, decision and next owner. Attributed revenue does not establish incremental lift; use a credible counterfactual only when the design and data permit it. Do not scale a tactic merely because its activity count increased.

The dashboard’s roadmap and 28-row backlog are the structured working versions. Every private metric remains **Requires Internal Access** until verified source data is connected.
