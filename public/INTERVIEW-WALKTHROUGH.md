# Conversation with Mike — 5–8 minutes

This is a preparation guide. Adapt the wording to your own voice and use only true personal examples. The dashboard demonstrates a work process; it does not manufacture experience or qualifications.

## 30-second opening

“I built a sample workspace for your Shopify client work: research an account, prepare an assessment, prioritize the next actions, and revisit the strategy. SEOasis is the public example here; client data would make the measurement and prioritization account-specific. The useful part is how I would organize the work inside your methodology and connect each decision to revenue.”

## Five-minute walkthrough

1. **0:00–0:40 · Methodology.** Explain the collection-led system, supporting structure, community presence, and quarterly learning. Attribute the methodology to SEOasis. Ask whether this accurately reflects current delivery.
2. **0:40–1:15 · Collections.** Show a client collection brief and the release gates. This is a client template; SEOasis itself is an agency. Explain why inventory, distinct intent, margin, and product-grid usefulness matter before page velocity.
3. **1:15–1:55 · Technical.** Show the bounded sample and one specific finding. The absence of canonical tags on sampled case pages is a consistency check, not proof of duplicate indexing. Explain how GSC/rendered validation would change the diagnosis.
4. **1:55–2:45 · Backlog.** Discuss three modest opportunities: a repeatable release QA pack; commercial-page discovery; and intent-role validation for nearby service pages. Open the evidence and dependency detail. Avoid narrating every tab.
5. **2:45–3:20 · Measurement.** Show exactly what requires access: GSC query/page behavior, GA4 landing-page funnels, Shopify economics, and Merchant Center diagnostics. Explain which decision each dataset informs.
6. **3:20–4:00 · Prioritization.** Show impact × confidence ÷ effort. Explain that dependencies and revenue economics can override an attractive score. Demonstrate a filter and an accountable owner/QA status.
7. **4:00–4:40 · First 90 days.** Baseline and align; ship a focused batch; QA and measure; then keep, expand, revise, or stop. Explain the Friday communication rhythm and quarterly client decision.
8. **4:40–5:20 · Contribution.** “This is the discipline I’d bring to your methodology: organized research, independent judgment, careful execution, and documented decisions. I’d want to learn your quality bar and make the fulfillment process easier to scale.”

If Mike wants to discuss a finding, follow that conversation. The artifact is a supporting tool, not a script that must be completed.

## Optional depth

- **Schema:** observed JSON-LD versus syntax, semantic correctness, and search-feature eligibility. No special schema guarantees AI inclusion; multi-product collections are not single-product rich-result pages.
- **Intent ownership:** nearby service URLs are a hypothesis to test with query/page exports and search results. Do not diagnose cannibalization from slugs alone.
- **Organic Shopping:** feed truth and eligibility first, controlled title/image testing second. The founder interview and public case study provide context; client results need internal validation.
- **AEO:** manually test a fixed query set, record platform/model/locale/date/conditions, preserve outputs and citations, and repeat. No platform visibility percentage is claimed here.
- **Operations:** demonstrate the full collection template, dependencies, partner handoff, QA acceptance criteria, and the local export.
- **Assistant (optional, 30 seconds):** open Ask workspace and ask about the current section. Show the evidence cards and their sources, then explain that optional local AI uses the same research as context. It has no private account access or live metrics. Enable and load the local model before the meeting if demonstrating generation; keep the five-minute path focused on the operating judgment.

## Five questions to ask Mike

1. When a catalog can support many collections, how do you balance page velocity with product overlap, inventory, and QA capacity?
2. What distinguishes a strong Friday update here, and which account decisions should the strategist make independently?
3. Where do blog and link-building partners fit today relative to the current collection-led offer?
4. Which evidence do you trust when visibility rises but revenue stays flat, and how do you define incremental lift?
5. What would I need to demonstrate in the first 90 days to earn responsibility for onboarding the next operator?

## Likely questions and useful responses

**Why audit our agency website when we work on ecommerce stores?**
“Your agency site gave me a specific public example for the research and evidence trail. The client frameworks show how I would organize Shopify account assessments, meeting preparation, and periodic reviews inside your methodology. Performance tracking would need authorized client data; this demo has no live integrations.”

**Are these scores real performance scores?**
“No. They show what this public sample can substantiate under four disclosed checks per category. The rubric and evidence are visible. I would replace uncertainty with account evidence, not treat the score as a diagnosis.”

**Can you prove this will increase revenue?**
“Not from public data. I can define the hypothesis, baseline, execution, QA, and decision rule. Attribution, seasonality, inventory, promotions, and margin need to be part of the measurement plan.”

**Why not build more pages immediately?**
“I would use your established cadence once we confirm intent differentiation, catalog fit, and QA capacity. I would start with a batch that lets us learn quickly and scale the patterns that deserve it.”

**How much of this did AI do?**
“AI helped parallelize research and implementation. I would own source checking, scope decisions, technical validation, copy editing, and the final recommendation. The source trail and limitations make that work inspectable.”

**What would you change first?**
“I would confirm your current scope and quality bar, then baseline one account and take responsibility for a small complete delivery cycle. The public-site observations are useful examples, but the client’s commercial data should determine the work order.”

**What would make you change your mind?**
“Evidence that the intended page is already serving the query well, that product or margin constraints make expansion unattractive, or that a simpler implementation can achieve the same outcome. I’d record the decision and revise the plan.”

**Have you done this before?**
Answer from your actual experience. Use one real example with your role, action, result, and what you learned. Do not imply the illustrative client framework is a prior client engagement.
