# Research method and synthesis trail

Research date: 14 October 2026 (UTC).

The objective is to demonstrate how an SEO Strategist would research, prioritize, execute, QA, measure, communicate, and revise work inside SEOasis’s public methodology. The agency website is a public evidence sample; Shopify collection, Shopping, revenue, and fulfillment sections are reusable client frameworks.

## Independent workstreams

A — company/methodology and job; B — technical; C — on-page; D — keywords/intent; E — Shopify collections; F — architecture/internal links; G — schema; H — AEO; I — content; J — authority/PR; K — Reddit; L — Shopping; M — CRO; N — competitors; O — measurement; P — strategy synthesis; Q — frontend/UX; R — independent QA.

Specialists first produced separate findings and source records. Given four concurrency slots, independent research ran in batches while the lead implemented the application. Strategy synthesis reviews the completed evidence, reconciles overlapping findings and contradictions, and produces a prioritized backlog and relative 90-day plan. Independent reviewers then inspect claims and product behavior. The QA report records observed checks and limitations, not a promise of future perfection.

## Evidence collection

1. Locate the employer job description and record hiring-status limitations.
2. Review the homepage, public services, collection methodology, case studies, blog, team, founder commentary, Reddit offering, and Shopping references.
3. Inspect robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Audit a purposive sample of 20 URLs with serial HTTP GET requests at least 1.1 seconds apart; parse response status, final URL, title, meta, H1/headings, canonical, robots directives, links, image attributes and JSON-LD.
4. Compare primary service/positioning pages from seven plausible ecommerce/Shopify SEO peers. The positioning map is an inferred, ordinal editorial model, not market performance.
5. Use official Google, Shopify, platform and community documentation for implementation frameworks and current eligibility constraints.
6. Preserve URL, page title, UTC access date and a short supporting note for each source. Link each material finding to its evidence and confidence.

## Interpretive rules

Company outcomes are attributed public claims. HTTP access is not indexation. Nearby slugs are not proof of cannibalization. Structured-data syntax is not rich-result eligibility. Readiness is not actual AI visibility. Referral or mention changes are not proof of causality. Revenue attribution is not necessarily incremental revenue.

Unavailable metrics use “Requires Internal Access” with the platform, metric, purpose and decision. No fabricated volumes, rankings, private analytics, revenue, link counts or model outputs are used. Suggested prompts are labeled as tests.

## Reconciliation and operating implications

The job’s blog/link partner responsibilities and the collection-led public service messaging are preserved as a scope clarification. Public narratives that emphasize different contributions to client results are not used to assign causality. Collection-page schema is separated from product rich-result implementation. Commercial intent ownership is validated before recommending consolidation or expansion. Technical findings are scoped to the sample.

The plan uses baseline → focused execution → QA → measurement → quarterly revision. The opportunity formula is impact × confidence ÷ effort; configurable exponent weights change the ranking transparently. Dependencies, account economics and delivery capacity remain judgment calls.

## Reproduce and inspect

`research/technical/audit_public.py` reproduces the bounded HTTP sample. Raw snapshots and complete page text remain local and are excluded from publication. `data/` holds concise structured research. `npm run research:compile` rebuilds the presentation bundle and source ledger. `npm run verify` checks data integrity and delivery coverage.
