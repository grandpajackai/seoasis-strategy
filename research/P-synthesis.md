# Strategy Lead review — 14 October 2026

The proposed operating model fits SEOasis’s public methodology: transactional keyword clustering → viable Shopify collections → accurate schema and useful links → complementary community/Shopping work → monitored results → quarterly recalibration. The interview should demonstrate ownership of that sequence, with explicit release gates and decisions. This is a proposed way to operate in the seat, not an independent validation of client results.

## Review coverage

Reviewed all A–O datasets: company and role, technical sample, representative pages/on-page, intent clusters, client collections, architecture, schema, AEO, content, authority, community, organic Shopping, CRO, competitors and measurement. The synthesis files contain 10 explainable scorecards, 5 strengths, 5 priority themes, 5 internal-validation areas, 28 deduplicated backlog items and 16 accountable roadmap tasks. Each material record retains source IDs, evidence, classification and confidence.

## Executive decisions

1. **Protect release quality.** Batch a few modest observed editorial/template checks, then independently inspect published output. These are clarity and process demonstrations, not claims of material traffic loss. **High confidence; OPPORTUNITY.** Sources: B-14, B-22, SRC-DI-INP, SRC-DI-PARAMETERS.
2. **Select collections that deserve to exist.** Use distinct buyer intent, an actual in-stock assortment, commercial constraints and the existing-page decision before creating URLs. Gate the first cohort rather than assume a universal page quota. **High confidence in the process; OPPORTUNITY.** Sources: SRC-E-HOME, SRC-E-EXPERT, SRC-E-PRODUCT.
3. **Turn measurement into a decision.** Agree revenue/channel definitions, save cohort baselines and release dates, repeat AI observations, then keep, expand, revise or stop each hypothesis at the quarter review. **High confidence in the process; OPPORTUNITY.** Sources: src-company-home, SRC-O-GA4, SRC-O-SHOPIFY, SRC-O-ATTRIBUTION.

| Title counts differed between workstreams | Report **7/20 exact duplicated suffixes**. The broader count of titles mentioning the brand twice includes natural case-subject wording and is not an issue count. This is repeated wording within a title, not duplicate titles across URLs. |

## Scoring and prioritization

Each score is **public evidence coverage**, with exactly four disclosed binary checks worth 25 points each. It is not a business-performance grade. An unchecked item means unverified in this study. Ecommerce architecture scores the documented client method; technical/on-page scores the agency HTML sample; authority and measurement explicitly reflect available public corroboration/documentation. These scopes must remain visible when presenting the scores.

Backlog impact and effort are relative 1–5 judgments, not forecasts or workday estimates. The configurable rank is impact × confidence ÷ effort. P0 is a prerequisite gate; P1 is focused execution; P2 is bounded expansion; P3 is an optional strategic bet. Dependencies and client constraints can override rank; a high mathematical score is not authorization to skip a gate. Confidence describes evidence/process fit, not the probability of a promised revenue lift.

## Private-data decisions

GSC determines actual indexation and query ownership. Shopify/finance determine assortment, stock and profitable categories. GA4/Shopify reconciliation determines comparable purchase and net-sales outcomes. Merchant Center determines feed eligibility and free-listing evidence. Saved AI/community/link records determine which visibility hypotheses can be supported. Each UI record states **Requires Internal Access**, platform, metric, purpose and affected decision.
