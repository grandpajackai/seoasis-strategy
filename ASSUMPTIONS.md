# Evidence boundaries and assumptions

Research snapshot: 14 October 2026, UTC. This is an independent candidate work sample using SEOasis as a public-data case study. SEOasis has not commissioned or approved it.

## Evidence classifications

- **OBSERVED:** directly inspected website/source content or a response element. A company’s performance claim can be observed as a claim; the underlying result is not independently verified.
- **INFERRED:** an analyst conclusion from observable evidence, with uncertainty stated.
- **OPPORTUNITY:** a proposed test, framework, workflow, or implementation idea.
- **REQUIRES INTERNAL DATA:** a conclusion or metric that cannot be established from this public review. The interface displays “Requires Internal Access” and identifies platform, metric, purpose, and decision.

Confidence describes the support for a conclusion, not the size of the commercial outcome. High means direct, specific evidence; Medium means a bounded interpretation; Low means an untested hypothesis or substantial missing context.

## What was not available

No access to SEOasis or client GSC, GA4, Shopify, Merchant Center, Asana, paid SEO databases, Google Ads, CRM, margin files, private Slack, or AI-visibility accounts. No private metrics are synthesized. Keyword volume, rankings, backlink counts, traffic history, conversion rate, client revenue attribution, AI visibility percentages, and incremental lift are unavailable unless explicitly described as an attributed public claim.

## Scope and technical limits

The HTTP sample covers 20 representative URLs chosen from public navigation and sitemap inventory. It is not a complete crawl. Robots and sitemap responses were inspected separately. Requests were serialized with at least 1.1 seconds between sample pages.

The technical dataset reflects server-delivered HTML. A 200 status, crawl permission, a sitemap entry, or a canonical tag does not prove Google indexation. JavaScript rendering, mobile behavior of the case-study website, full link-target health, complete crawl depth, and field Core Web Vitals require further testing. No site-wide orphan, broken-link, duplication, or cannibalization claim follows from the sample.

JSON parsing confirms syntax only. Google Rich Results and Schema.org semantic validation are separate tasks. Schema does not guarantee rich results, model access, citations, or recommendations. Product rich-result guidance concerns eligible product pages, not an arbitrary multi-product collection. FAQ markup must match visible content and current platform guidance; do not add it for a promised search enhancement. Current eligibility is recorded in the schema source review.

## Scoring

The ten executive scores measure public evidence coverage. Each has four disclosed binary criteria worth 25 points. Different categories have explicit scope: agency-site implementation, public methodology, or available measurement evidence. They are not mutually comparable business KPIs, an overall agency grade, or a substitute for an account baseline. An unmet check means “not substantiated by this review,” not “the capability does not exist.”

## Strategic assumptions

Collection, Shopping, CRO, and fulfillment templates are for client ecommerce accounts. SEOasis itself is an agency; its own site has a qualified-lead funnel. Hypothetical collection briefs are clearly labeled and are not prior client engagements. Owners, statuses, deadlines, and priorities are proposed roles and relative windows, not real assignments.

The intended use is a reusable workspace for client research, assessments, meeting preparation, and periodic account reviews. SEOasis supplies the public example, rather than a live client account. Performance tracking would require authorized platform connections or account exports; neither is provided by this demo.

Commercial-page overlap is a hypothesis. Query/page exports, SERP intent, conversions, and intended page roles are needed before choosing differentiation, consolidation, redirects, or new-page creation. Collection expansion depends on relevant products, stock, margin, distinct intent, maintainability, and QA capacity. No guaranteed page, ranking, AI, or revenue outcome is promised.

The job description includes blog and link-building partner coordination; current public service copy emphasizes collection-led delivery and sometimes disclaims blogs/backlinks. Homepage diagrams also include blog spokes. This is a scope question for Mike, not evidence of an undocumented policy change.

## Job availability and source freshness

The public employer LinkedIn description was accessible. The old Gusto posting returned HTTP 410 and the company board did not confirm an open role. The artifact maps the observed role description but does not assert that hiring remains active. Historical/unavailable source URLs remain in the source ledger with their retrieval limitation.

Public pages and guidelines can change after the snapshot. Sources retain titles, URLs, access dates, and supporting notes. Revalidate implementation guidance before using it on a production account.

## Local working data

Backlog edits, priority settings, collection briefs, theme preference, and manual AI notes stay in browser localStorage. They are not connected to client platforms. Manual AI notes are user-entered and unverified; the research contains no tested model-output dataset. Clear or export browser data as appropriate before sharing a device.
